## 2. Partner universe (scored)

**Ideal partner:** same buyer (founder/CFO/ops at SMBs $1M–50M), *adjacent in the workflow* (before/after/alongside), complementary not competitive, SMB-scalable enough to say yes to a small partner.

**Decision rule at this stage:** pursue partners who are *small enough to need content and say yes*, not big enough to ignore you. Big names become reachable once the accountant channel or a case study exists (Q3).

## 3. Top three plays (all $0, all no-face)

### Play A — "The client spend review" co-branded offer (accounting practice tools, Q2)
*Co-marketing layer on the existing accountant affiliate channel.*

- **Asset:** fCMO co-writes a guide *for accountants* with one practice tool as co-brand — "The client spend review: a new service for your practice" (why clients leak money, how to run a review in an afternoon, how n4ma proves findings). CTA: the free audit.
- **Lead handling:** partner intro credit flows through the §7 tracked link (leads to n4ma; partner earns the flat $50 CAD/confirmed purchase + looks like the hero to their client).
- **Why it works:** the partner needs content for their accountant audience; the accountant needs a service to sell; n4ma needs the accountant's trusted voice. Everyone's incentive aligns — no cash changes hands.

### Play B — Contract-workflow content swap (e-sign / contract lifecycle partner, Q2–Q3)
- **Asset:** co-authored piece — "What to check when you sign a vendor contract (and what to watch after)" — the natural hand-off story: *they* help the SMB sign safely; *n4ma* keeps watching renewals, cancellation windows, and escalations after the signature.
- **Format:** guest-post swap or joint guide (each keeps own leads, link exchange). No gating — distribution is the goal, not list capture, at this audience size.
- **Idea prompt used:** "What would we create if we had to launch something in 2 weeks?" → this is it: one honest, useful asset both audiences need.

### Play C — Marketplace listing applications (Q3+, long game)
- **Targets, in order:** **Xero App Store** (category: subscription management/spend) → **Google Workspace Marketplace** (Gmail/Drive OAuth apps already exist) → **Slack App Directory** (Slack OAuth app exists) once it passes review.
- **Prep now (cheap):** listing copy from the landing page, screenshots from the launch film stills, link to privacy/security pages (exist). Submit Q3; follow up Q4.
- **Why:** built-in discovery inside the exact stack the ICP uses — the "App Store distribution" play, at SMB scale. Low effort, slow payoff, zero CAC.

**Back pocket (Weeks 9–12, once the content hub exists):** ghostwritten guest essay in 2 SMB-finance newsletters ("Your business is leaking money — here's how to find it") — permissionless audience borrowing, no face required.

## 4. Cold outreach templates (adapted to zero-audience honesty)

**To an accounting practice tool (Play A):**
```
Subject: n4ma + [Tool]: a client spend review offer for your accountants

Hi [Name],

I'm the founder of n4ma — the AI financial watchdog that finds money
SMBs are wasting on software subscriptions and contracts, with every
finding tied back to evidence.

Your audience (accountants) sits directly between SMBs and their spend
— and we share them exactly: our free audit is the natural tool for an
accountant to run a client spend review. I'd like to co-write a guide
for your audience — "The client spend review: a new service for your
practice" — co-branded, with your tool as the workflow partner. We do
the writing; you get a genuinely useful asset for your content calendar.

Worth a 15-minute call to see if it fits?

[Founder name]
```

**To an e-sign / contract partner (Play B):**
```
Subject: n4ma + [Tool]: after the signature

Hi [Name],

I run n4ma — we watch SMBs' signed vendor contracts for renewals,
cancellation windows, and price escalations after they're signed.

That's a natural continuation of what [Tool] does: you help companies
sign safely; we make sure the contract doesn't quietly bite them later.
I'd love to co-author a piece with your team — "What to check when you
sign a vendor contract (and what to watch after)" — swapped on both our
blogs/newsletters. We draft; you edit; both audiences get something
useful.

Open to a quick call?

[Founder name]
```

**Prepare before any call:** 2–3 concrete ideas (above), the free audit as the demo, honesty about audience size (reciprocity = effort + a great asset, not reach), and the tracked-link arrangement for Play A.

## 6. Success metrics

- **Quantitative (per partner):** audit starts via tracked link; partner-content → audit conversion; confirmed Team Plus purchases attributed; newsletter/blog referral clicks.
- **Qualitative:** ease of collaboration, partner responsiveness, audience reception, brand fit — and whether the relationship is worth repeating.
- **Kill criterion:** a partner who doesn't promote after the asset ships gets no second campaign. Promotion commitments are agreed up front (per the co-marketing agreement outline in the skill).
