# N4MA — Sales Enablement Kit (founder-led)

**Skill:** `sales-enablement` v2.0.1 · **Date:** 2026-09-04
**Motion:** founder sells. Two surfaces: (a) Team Plus self-serve with email follow-up (reply SLA 24h — §8 Move 3), (b) Business/Enterprise sales-led (custom, contact email). Collateral below arms the founder; nothing ships to a prospect until it has real proof points (no fabricated customers).

## 1. One-pager — "How n4ma calculates savings" (plan §8 Move 5)

Purpose: the trust document (methodology as sales material) + leave-behind. Structure:
1. **Problem (1 sentence):** SMBs leak money through renewals, escalations, unused seats — nobody watches.
2. **The watch:** n4ma connects to Gmail/Drive/Slack/upload; monitors contracts and invoices continuously.
3. **How findings are made:** AI extraction (renewal dates, deadlines, escalations, spend) + deterministic rules (window math, price deltas, duplication) — every finding links to its source clause/document/page.
4. **How savings are estimated:** stated methodology — each estimate labeled (low–high range, evenly spread, no growth assumed, per the dashboard's own footer); never presented as guaranteed.
5. **3 differentiators:** evidence-first (not AI vibes) · bring-your-own-system (4 real sources) · no-subscription one-time $250 CAD (nothing auto-charges).
6. **Proof:** free audit link + "see it on your own data in 2 minutes" (the demo IS the proof).
7. **CTA + contact:** founder name, email, phone optional. One page, front only.

## 3. Demo script (30–45 min, founder)

1. **Opening (2 min):** "You said renewals surprise you / prices went up — let me show you what n4ma does with one contract." No product tour.
2. **Discovery recap (3 min):** confirm their stack + pain (which sources they'd connect, last surprise).
3. **Walkthrough (15–20 min, 3 workflows):** (a) the 2-minute audit on *their* document if shareable — else a sample; (b) a finding → evidence trail (the trust moment); (c) the watch: renewal window + what happens when it moves (digest/weekly).
4. **Interaction points:** ask mid-demo — "Which of your vendors would this have caught first?"
5. **Close (5 min):** next step + price path (Team Plus one-time; or Business scoping). Always end on their data, not features.
