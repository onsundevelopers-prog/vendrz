# N4MA — Cold Outreach Set (accountants, partners, backers, first users)

**Skills:** `cold-email` v2.0.0 (+ prospecting folded in) · **Date:** 2026-09-04
**Voice rules (per the skill):** peer not vendor; every sentence earns its place; personalization must connect to the problem; one low-friction ask; subject = 2–4 lowercase words, internal-looking; 3–5 emails per sequence with new angles, then a breakup email. All sends personally from the founder (no tool-spam feel); B2B weekdays; volume is deliberately low (10–25 per list) because each is semi-personalized.

**Sender:** founder, `hello@n4ma.online` (verified domain once RESEND key is in).

---

## List 1 — Accountants & fractional CFOs (Q2, feeds plan §7 + co-marketing Play A)

*Target: solo/small practices with SMB clients; the observation is their client churn is tied to hidden spend surprises.*

**Subject:** client spend reviews

**Email 1 — observation → offer → low-friction ask**
```
Hi {first name},

Clients don't fire accountants over a missing tax deadline — they leave when a
renewal or price increase surprises them and they feel nobody was watching.

I built n4ma for that gap: it watches a client's software contracts and finds
missed cancellation windows, price increases, and duplicate tools — with every
finding tied back to the actual document. A review takes ~2 minutes, no signup.

For practices, it turns into a service: run a spend review for each client,
present the findings, look like the hero. Partners earn $50 CAD per confirmed
client purchase — and the finding is the proof, so it sells itself.

Want me to send a one-page version you can read in two minutes?

{Founder} — n4ma
```
**Email 2 (+5d, new angle — the tool itself):**
```
Subject: your clients' renewals

Hi {first name}, — following up on the spend-review idea. The fastest way to
judge it: run your own firm's stack through the free audit (two minutes, no
signup) and see what it surfaces. If it finds nothing, we're wrong for you.
[Run the free audit]

If it finds something — that's the demo you'd give a client.
```
**Email 3 (+10d, breakup):**
```
Subject: closing the loop

Hi {first name}, — last note. I'll stop emailing after this. If client spend
reviews ever become a service you want to offer, n4ma is set up to be the tool
underneath it, and the $50-per-client commission stands. Reply any time.
```

## Sequence mechanics (all lists)

| Email | Delay | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Observation + value + low-friction ask |
| 2 | +5d | New angle: proof (free audit demo / draft asset) |
| 3 | +10d | Breakup: leave value, keep door open |

**Tracking:** plain links with UTM (no spy pixels on first send — trust-first); replies land in the founder inbox; log outcomes (replied / meeting / no) in the outreach sheet. **Metrics:** reply rate ≥20% on the accountant list is good at this volume; convert replies to 15-min calls, calls to 3–5 active partners by Q3 (plan §7 target).

## Personalization fields to fill per send
{first name}, one specific signal about *their* practice/company (client size, a recent post, their niche), and — for accountants — the phrase they'd use for this ("clean-up work," "advisory services"). The cold-email skill's rule: if the personalized opening can be deleted and the email still works, it isn't personalized enough.
