# N4MA — Social Calendar (no-face, LinkedIn + X + Shorts)

**Skill:** `social` v2.2.0 · **Date:** 2026-09-04
**Constraint (locked):** no founder face/voice-persona. Everything is **screen capture + captions/VO + text posts**. That's not a limitation for this product — the screen IS the story (findings, evidence, dashboards).
**Cadence:** 2 posts/wk per platform (LinkedIn + X) + 1 short-form cut/wk (Shorts/Reels, already produced from the film). Volume over polish-perfect: batching 2–3h/wk per the skill.

## The no-face content atom system
One pillar piece → many atoms (social skill repurposing): every demo video is already captioned (shorts have burned-in text — required, most watch muted); every blog piece ships with 2–3 standalone subheads + 1 stat (artifact 07 discipline). Weekly digest findings (artifact 02 Flow D) generate the **finding-of-the-week** post automatically — real data, real proof, honest.

**Hook rules (skill):** first line stops the scroll — value/contrarian hooks fit the voice ("Most companies find out about an auto-renewal the day it charges. n4ma finds out 30 days early."); no hype, no "revolutionary," no emoji in copy.

## Engagement routine (30 min/day, founder or fCMO with founder approval)
1. Reply to all comments (5 min) — first hour matters on Shorts.
2. 5–10 quality comments on target accounts: accountants/fractional-CFOs, SMB-finance voices, spend-tool category (10–20 tracked accounts list) (15 min).
3. Share/repost with added insight + 2–3 DMs to new relevant connections (10 min).
**Listening (weekly):** "auto-renewal," "cancellation window," "software we don't use" mentions; competitor threads; the accountant/CFO posts to comment on (artifact 06 Mode 2 sources double as listening targets).

## Metrics & weekly review (30 min/wk)
Top/bottom 3 posts, follower growth, engagement rate, and — the only conversion that matters — **clicks → audit starts (UTM)**. Reach/impressions are context, not goals. Posting times adjusted from data; if reach declines, engage more + avoid external links in post body (skill guidance) — the audit link goes in comments/first-reply.

## Owners
Demos + shorts + finding-of-the-week: fCMO (edits exist); founder approves voice + posts (account is the founder's). Batch on Friday for the week ahead; keep 1–2wk queue.
