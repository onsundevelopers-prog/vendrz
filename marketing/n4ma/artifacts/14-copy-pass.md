## Recurring issues found & fixed (examples)

| Issue | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-ism opener** | "I hope this email finds you well." | "Hi {first name}," — straight in |
| **Vague promise** | "n4ma surfaces opportunities to reduce costs." | "n4ma found your {vendor} renewal — window closes {date}." (specific, evidence-first) |
| **Jargon stacking** | "leverage our evidence-backed intelligence platform" | "every finding links to the exact clause — verify it in seconds" |
| **Feature dump** | List of 8 features as the pitch | One proof point: the finding + the evidence |
| **Pressure CTA** | "Book your demo today!" | "Worth a 20-minute call this week?" (low-friction ask) |
| **Passive voice** | "Savings estimates are calculated by the system…" | "Every estimate is labeled with its method — nothing is presented as guaranteed." (active, honest) |
| **Double messaging** | Trial + purchase CTAs in one email | One job per email (02 discipline): trial-ender sells continuation; expiry email sells the keep |
| **Em-dash overuse** | Multiple em dashes per sentence | Cut to one per paragraph max; commas where the voice is calmer |

## Voice checklist (applied to every artifact; reusable for all future copy)

1. Lowercase "n4ma" in prose; display copy may use spec headlines.
2. No emoji in body copy anywhere (artifacts + live UI).
3. Every dollar figure labeled (estimate / illustrative / one-time $250 CAD — never "savings guaranteed").
4. Every claim traceable: findings → evidence; features → what they do for the reader.
5. One CTA per email/post; button text = action + outcome ("Start your first import", not "Click here").
6. Sentences readable aloud; ~2 short paragraphs max between headers in email.
7. Zero fabrication: no fake testimonials, no invented customers, no scraped-looking numbers.
8. AI-writing scan: no "I hope this finds you", "delve", "leverage", "best-in-class", "seamlessly", "empower", no 3+-em-dash runs, no filler adverbs ("genuinely/actually/literally" — used once where it earns the place).
